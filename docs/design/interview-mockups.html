Retry with backoff for REST tasks

Decisions

  • Retry all methods; POST only with an Idempotency-Key header (Q1, round 1 · round 2).
  • At most 5 attempts, and never more than 120 s in total (Q2 · your note · round 2).
  • Exponential backoff with full jitter, base 200 ms (Q3, guess kept).
  • Retry-After honoured on 429 and 503, capped by the 120 s budget.

Design

One RetryPolicy in the shared REST client…
